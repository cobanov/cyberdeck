Files

Creality K1, PLA, 0.2 mm layers, 3 walls, 20 % gyroid, no brim. The lid needs supports under its two hinge knuckles; the slate needs none. In both the phone drops into a pocket and MagSafe holds it: stick a steel MagSafe ring sticker into the round recess in the pocket's floor.

Every measurement is a named parameter in Python scripts that build the models in FreeCAD. To fit another phone or keyboard, change the numbers and run them again: see Customizing.
