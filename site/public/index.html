A pocket deck you can print.

An iPhone 12 mini and a Torima M7 mini Bluetooth keyboard in a 3D-printed shell: a clamshell with a hinged lid, or a one-piece slate. Turn the models here and download the STL files.

Files

Creality K1, PLA, 0.2 mm layers, 3 walls, 20 % gyroid, no brim. Only the lid needs supports, under its two hinge knuckles. Assembly, hardware and print notes are in the README.

Every measurement is a named parameter in Python scripts that build the models in FreeCAD. To fit another phone or keyboard, change the numbers and run them again: see Customizing.
