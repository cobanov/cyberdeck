The SSH terminal for your cyberdeck.

DeckTerm turns an iPhone and any hardware keyboard into a pocket SSH terminal. Built for landscape, driven from the keyboard.

Coming soon to the App Store

Support and questions

Free, with an optional tip jar. For iPhone with iOS 17 or later.

Built for any deck

An iPhone and a hardware keyboard, held sideways: that is a deck. A 3D-printed shell, a stand behind a mechanical board or a folding travel keyboard, over Bluetooth or a cable.

What DeckTerm does

  • Keyboard first

    Your servers on one screen. ⌘1–⌘9 connects, ⌘W goes back, and a thin key bar adds the Esc, Ctrl and arrow keys small keyboards lack.

  • Private

    No account, no tracking, no servers of its own. Passwords and your SSH key stay in this iPhone's Keychain.

  • Safe connections

    Sign in with a password or an OpenSSH key. Host keys are pinned on first connect, and a changed key is refused.
